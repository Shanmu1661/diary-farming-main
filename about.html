Our Purpose & Compassion

Happy Cows Make Wholesome Milk

At Daily Product, dairy farming is not an industrial assembly line — it is an ecosystem of care. Our 350+ indigenous Gir cows and Murrah buffaloes graze freely across 280 acres of organic green alfalfa and Napier grass.

We operate under a strict Zero Hormone & Zero Antibiotic pledge. Calves always drink their share of milk first before any milking occurs. Our cows sleep on rubber comfort mats, listen to soothing acoustic music during automated touchless milking, and receive 24/7 on-farm veterinary care.

Certified 100% Organic

Zero synthetic chemical fertilizers or pesticides on our grazing fields.

Compassionate Care

Non-milking geriatric cattle live peacefully in our farm retirement sanctuary.
