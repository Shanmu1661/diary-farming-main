Virtual Farm Experience

From Our Soil to Your Doorstep

Step inside our 280-acre solar-powered organic dairy farm. See how humane animal husbandry meets cutting-edge cold-chain engineering.

Chapter 01 • Green Fields

Pasture-Raised With Certified Organic Fodder

The quality of milk begins with what the cow eats. Unlike commercial feedlot cattle fed synthetic grain concentrates and animal by-products, our indigenous Gir cows roam in lush pastures grown with natural vermicompost.

  • 100% pesticide-free green alfalfa, oat grass, and sweet sorghum
  • Pure borehole mountain water enriched with natural minerals
  • Free-range roaming for at least 8 hours every day in sunlight
Chapter 02 • Automated Milking

Zero Human Touch Milking Parlor

Hand milking can introduce perspiration, dust, and airborne bacteria. Our food-grade computerized herringbone milking stalls automatically sanitize teats, gently extract milk via pulsation mimicking a calf's nursing, and pump it through hermetically sealed stainless steel conduits.

  • Calming acoustic tunes playing to reduce cow stress and boost oxytocin naturally
  • Zero contact with air until you unseal the bottle in your kitchen
  • Calves nurse first: 25% of maternal milk is reserved exclusively for the offspring
Chapter 03 • Cold Science

Flash Chilling to 4°C in 15 Minutes

Freshly milked milk emerges at cow body temperature (around 37°C), which is the prime breeding ground for bacteria if left at ambient temperatures. Our high-velocity plate heat exchangers crash the temperature down to 3.8°C within 15 minutes of extraction.

  • Halts bacterial propagation completely without requiring ultra-high heat pasteurization
  • 26 on-site biochemical purity tests for every morning batch
  • Certificates published directly to your subscriber dashboard before breakfast
Chapter 04 • Sustainability

100% Solar Powered & Circular Glass Cycle

Our dairy facility generates 100% of its electricity through a 150 kW rooftop solar array. Cow dung is converted into biogas for heating water and organic manure for fertilizing our green crops, creating a completely closed-loop regenerative ecosystem.
